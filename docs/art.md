# Arena artwork

`public/assets/arena.png` is an original 1536 × 1024 backdrop generated with the built-in image generation tool. It is used underneath the interactive canvas characters and effects. No existing game's assets, branding, or characters were used.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original landscape game arena background for Neon Hollow, a top-down 2D monster survival game; also usable behind a start screen. Generate exactly one image, 1536x1024 or similar landscape aspect ratio. Scene/backdrop: a dark sci-fi abandoned botanical courtyard at night, deep navy and teal stone floor tiles with cracked concrete, small bioluminescent mint plants and sparse weathered machinery concentrated around the edges, subtle amber pools of light. Style/medium: high-quality painterly pixel-art-adjacent game environment, rich tactile textures, restrained moody atmosphere. Composition/framing: strictly overhead orthographic view, flat playable floor across the image; center must be open and low contrast so game characters can remain readable. Environmental details mostly at perimeter. Lighting/mood: dark atmospheric night, delicate mint bioluminescence and restrained amber accents, deep navy shadows. Constraints: no text, no UI, no logos, no watermark, no characters, no creatures. Single finished backdrop only.
